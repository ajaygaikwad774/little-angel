Learning by Experience

Why Neuron Connections are Important?

Neuron connections decide the kind of response that a child gives to a particular stimuli.

The right kind of neuron connnections lead to a desired response in a child.

These responses become a habit and thereafter forms the personality of a child.

Eg - If there is a child with a running nose in the class. If the teacher refrains from shaming a child & simply helps the child to wipe the nose while carrying on with a rhyme or a story, the child learns to give a similar response. Not to ridicule but to empathize.

Critical Period

The formation of new neurons and synapses is fastest at birth then slows down over time, that is why babies and toddlers can learn so fast compared to adults.
