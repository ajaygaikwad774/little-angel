Ready To Fly I

(Age: 4-5 years)/Lower KG - Kindergarten

The Ready to Fly I program prepares children for the next level of socio-emotional development and academic, social, and physical skills. The levels of facilitating, learning, and participation are higher. We help them master reading and writing long and short vowels, blending, diagraphs, rhyming, and comprehension.

Ready To Fly II

(Age: 5-6 years)/Upper KG - Kindergarten

Designed for 5-6 years old, the Ready to Fly II program prepares children for formal schooling. Develops excellent reading skills through an emphasis on consonant blend sounds.
Children are introduced to 'Addition' & 'Subtraction' with the help of the concept 'One Less' & 'One More', ascending and descending and ordinal and cardinal numbers.
